Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. No ads, no paid tier.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. No servers, no iCloud sync, no account. See Privacy Policy for details.

Why don't HSK 2+ sentences have native audio?

To keep the app small (~50MB), only HSK 1 audio is bundled. HSK 2-7 sentences fall back to iOS TTS, or fetch native audio from CDN if that feature is enabled.

Some Vietnamese translations look off?

Some translations are machine-generated. We improve based on user feedback. Email us any obvious errors — they'll be fixed in the next update.

How do I delete all learning data?

Go to Settings → Clear learning data. Or uninstall the app.

What languages are supported?

UI: Vietnamese + English. Content: Simplified Chinese with Vietnamese + English translations.
