Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-29 · Version 1.0

Overview

Quick Chinese ("the app", "we") is a Chinese language learning app organized by HSK level. We respect your privacy and commit to not collecting personal data we don't need.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, or phone number.
  • No account signup required.
  • No third-party analytics (Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, etc.).
  • No location tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, or other iCloud data.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device (UserDefaults + cache), never synced to any server:

  • List of sentences you have bookmarked.
  • List of sentences you have marked as "learned".
  • Number of times each sentence was tested (for spaced repetition).
  • App settings (language, pinyin, sound, haptic).
  • Audio files cached locally (when the CDN feature is enabled).

You can delete all of this at any time via Settings → Clear learning data, or by uninstalling the app.

Network connections

The app connects to the internet only to download pronunciation audio files for HSK 2+ sentences (if the CDN feature is configured). No personal data is transmitted — only public audio file paths.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions beyond audio playback, which requires no manual grant.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. No adult content, no advertising, no external links except those on the Content Sources & Attributions page.

In-app purchases

If a Pro tier is offered in the future, transactions are handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your card details or Apple ID.

Policy changes

If we update this policy, the new version replaces the in-app copy and this page. The effective date updates accordingly.
